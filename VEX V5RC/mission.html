Missions That We Have

We mainly have 3 missions for the team. Below, you can see those missions list in order from the most important to less but still important.

Equality

One of our biggest pillar in builiding this team was to have equality among all of the team members and that all of the advices are listened and then a certain task is decided via voting.

Inspiration

Second most important mission that we had was to inspire other people by creating something unique which then thereby can inspire the people who might participate in future.

No Luck

The third most important mission that we had was to not rely ourselves on luck but rely ourselves on the accomplishment that we made after working hard along all those tough times that we faced.
