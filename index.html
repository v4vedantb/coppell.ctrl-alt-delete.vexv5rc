Our Brainstormed Ideas

During our first days, we brainstormed mainly three ideas for the creation of our bot. These designs were one fo the top rated by many experience people and so we thought to go with it.

C-Design

The C bot has 3 main components:
1.Drivetrain
2.Intake
3.Scoring Mechanism

S-Design

The S bot has 3 main components:
1.The front/bottom is the intake.
2.The mid section which stages the cubes.
3.The top which is the scoring mechanism

Track Design

The Track bot has 3 main components:
1.DriveTrain
2.Intake
3.Track
